graspable_carton -- a small upright carton, sized to be picked off a table by a narrow parallel
gripper; the sibling of `graspable_box`.

Original primitive geometry authored for roqsim (no third-party content). No meshes: the model is
MJCF primitives, so there is nothing here but our own geometry and inertial tuning.
