Modified for roqsim (its licence asks for changes to be indicated): the source's door was split into
its leaf (this model), its frame (door_frame) and its handle, which door_glass shares, and converted
to OBJ meshes and a MuJoCo model, which may simplify, scale, move and recolour them; the leaf is turned
to the door plugin's axes, and the handle is copied and mirrored onto the leaf's other face. The leaf
collides as its mesh's hull; the handle does not collide.
