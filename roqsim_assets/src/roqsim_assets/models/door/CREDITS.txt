Modified for roqsim (its licence asks for changes to be indicated): the source's door was split into
its leaf (this model), its frame (door_frame) and a glazed leaf (door_glass), and converted to OBJ
meshes and a MuJoCo model, which may simplify, scale, move and recolour them. The leaf collides as
its mesh's hull; its handle does not collide.
