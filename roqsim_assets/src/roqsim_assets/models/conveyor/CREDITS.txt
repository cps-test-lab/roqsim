conveyor -- a benchtop velocity-driven belt conveyor (invisible belt box on a slide joint, driven by
the `conveyor` plugin; visible belt and rollers are decorative).

Hand-authored for roqsim: MJCF primitives only, written by us. No third-party meshes, textures or
code are included, and nothing here is derived from an external model.
