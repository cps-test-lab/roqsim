Depot warehouse scene
=====================

Ported from the Open Robotics "Depot" model on Gazebo Fuel:
  https://fuel.gazebosim.org/1.0/OpenRobotics/models/Depot  (version 6)

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)

Authors: Rafi Abdullah, Louise Poubel (Open Robotics)

This is the same warehouse nav2's tb4_simulation_launch.py loads in Gazebo. The mesh + textures were
tessellated/baked to MuJoCo by roqsim_scenes/tools/{sdf_to_scene,scene_to_mjcf}.py; exact provenance
(URI, version, sha256) is pinned in assets.lock.json.
