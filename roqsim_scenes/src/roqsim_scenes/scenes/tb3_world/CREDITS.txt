TurtleBot3 World scene
======================

  https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/turtlebot3_simulations

  ("TurtleBot3 World", version 1.0, "World of TurtleBot3 with ROS symbol")

This is the hexagonal room with nine pillars that TurtleBot 3's own tutorials and nav2's TurtleBot 3
examples navigate. The files read by the import, and their sha256 at that commit:

  turtlebot3_gazebo/worlds/turtlebot3_world.world

    80d2cd15882457b35ed9bbe7fcf70153158bdae4260916c8227e2fe5c624239a
  turtlebot3_gazebo/models/turtlebot3_world/meshes/wall.dae
    da086329366d78b9ee73a8dc210232683bdfee93db66bf8ddd14b1bd545cf089
  turtlebot3_gazebo/models/turtlebot3_world/meshes/hexagon.dae
    16b1756c50e90c2161f89e2ada1047b69d495889c53522197520116de23a4cda

The commit and those hashes are the pin, and external/convert/build_tb3_world_scene.py checks them
before it imports anything -- the world's two <include> URIs (a Fuel ground plane and sun, both CC0)
are what assets.lock.json covers, because they are the only assets the importer fetches itself.

Geometry and poses are the source's, unchanged. What the port had to decide, and how the result was
checked against the occupancy grid ROBOTIS publishes for this world, is in port_log.md.
