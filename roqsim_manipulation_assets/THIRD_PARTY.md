(`models/<name>/<NAME>_LICENSE`: upstream, commit, copyright, what was changed, the full licence
text). Look there for the terms of a specific model; this file does not duplicate them.

When adding a model, keep its licence file and `CREDITS.txt` alongside it and list it here.
