Every file here is regenerated from that source by the import below;
none is edited by hand. Its walker.json is measured on the mesh against
the bones the walker's skin is posed on at runtime, and grounds each
shoe on its hull.

Imported with `roqsim walker import-actor`: the skin (mesh, textures,
skeleton, skin weights) is the source's. Locomotion comes from this
package's models/anims/, which are CARLA retargets under CC-BY 4.0 --
so this walker carries CARLA attribution too; see THIRD_PARTY.md.

The finger weights (*.fingers.npz) are the same source's, kept apart from
the wrist by the import; a walker with `hands: true` binds its fingers
with them.
