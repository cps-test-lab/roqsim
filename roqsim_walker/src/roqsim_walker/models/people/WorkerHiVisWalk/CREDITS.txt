A derivative of that actor, under the same licence: imported with `roqsim walker
import-actor` (the skin -- mesh, texture, skeleton, skin weights -- is the source's), then
changed by `roqsim walker derive-hivis-worker`: the surgical mask, its ties and the name
badge removed and the mouth closed, the mesh subdivided once, and the texture repainted
(work shirt with a high-visibility vest, work trousers, safety shoes, hair, mouth).
Its pelvis height, shoe soles and collision radii are measured on the derived mesh.
Every file here is regenerated by that tool from the import; none is edited by hand.
Locomotion comes from this package's models/anims/, which are CARLA retargets under
CC-BY 4.0 -- so this walker carries CARLA attribution too; see THIRD_PARTY.md.
